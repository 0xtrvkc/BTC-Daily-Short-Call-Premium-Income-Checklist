2. Calculate IV/HV Ratio (v2: HV30, ratio ≥ 1.15) coinglass.com/options/Bybit + TradingView ▼
What to do: Get IV from Bybit option chain (% column next to each strike). Get HV30 from TradingView → add indicator "Historical Volatility" length 30. Then calculate the ratio.

— IV (Implied Volatility) = what the market fears BTC will do. Baked into option price.
— HV30 (Historical Volatility, 30-day) = what BTC actually did over the past 30 days.
— The ratio tells you how much the market is overpaying vs reality. That overpayment = your edge.

IV/HV Ratio = IV ÷ HV30 Example: IV = 31%, HV30 = 19.55% → Ratio = 31/19.55 = 1.59 ✅ Sweet spot Ratio < 1.00 → SKIP — selling cheap, no edge at all Ratio 1.00–1.15 → Borderline — edge too small, be careful Ratio 1.15–1.50 → OK — normal edge, proceed Ratio > 1.50 → Sweet Spot — strong edge, vol spike opportunity
→Ratio ≥ 1.50: Sweet spot — maximum edge
→Ratio 1.15–1.50: OK — proceed normally
→Ratio 1.00–1.15: Borderline — reduce size, extra caution
→Ratio < 1.00 (IV < HV): SKIP — no edge, tick below
v2 change: use HV30 (not HV7) and check the ratio, not just the gap. Hard skip threshold is ratio < 1.15.
